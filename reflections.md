This mission helped me to understand and learned the differences about (AWS ) Amazon Web Services , Micrisoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP). Among the three  platforms, Google cloud impressed me most because of its strong capabilities in (AI) Artificial Intilligence, Machine Learning and data analytics. I also learned that each of cloud provider has an different strengths, which means that most provider is not necessarily an best choice for every organization. AWS, Azure, and GCP have a many similarities. But all these three platforms provide virtual machines, object storage, networking, identity and access management, databases, Kubernetes services, monitoring, security features, and other cloud services. They have also use global infrastructure that allows organizations to deploy applications in different geographic locations. Although the services perform similar functions, their names, features, management tools, and integrations can be different.
